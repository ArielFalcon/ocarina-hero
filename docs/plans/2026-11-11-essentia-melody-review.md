# Plan review

## Initial independent reviews

The comprehension review identified a high-severity gap in target-motif acceptance: the plan allowed delivery as an evaluated candidate without a criterion establishing that it followed the intended violin line.

The simplicity/readiness review identified the same quality gate as a medium-severity readiness issue. It otherwise confirmed that the separate isolated default is supported by observed regressions and that Worker bootstrap, lifecycle ownership and cleanup are proportionate.

## Batched correction

The revised plan requires an explicit auditory verdict from the user or an independent musical reviewer, comparing the original 0–13 s clip with the unadapted neutral rendering of the actual candidate events, before production edits. It defines usable correspondence in terms of the intended melodic line, phrase/note order, rhythm and pauses, and rejects identified missing passages or a wrong line. Silence is not approval and sparse anchors are not complete ground truth.

An unavailable or failed auditory gate stops the integration with the current app unchanged. A technically working but musically unusable mode cannot be reported as the completed improvement. The browser result must also reproduce the accepted source notes and timing within documented tolerances before adaptation. A different model or separation stage requires a new proposal.

## Scoped recheck

The fresh independent scoped recheck confirmed that the quality-gate finding is resolved and identified no concrete blocker introduced by the correction. The pre-integration auditory verdict, stop condition and browser consistency check are explicit. This does not approve implementation or delivery. The user's musical verdict and approval of the concrete mode/default plan remain pending; no production edits have started.
