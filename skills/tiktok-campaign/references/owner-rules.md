# Owner rules for every film

Collected from the owner's reviews (November 2026). Add every new general rule here, with the owner's words.

## Songs

- Every song must be instantly recognisable to a general audience, not only the opener.
- The largest text while a song plays is the name people know it by ("Tetris", not "Music A"; "Nokia ringtone", not "Grande Valse"). Headline "Learn the Tetris song" with the subtitle "in Zelda Ocarina of Time".
- Each song stays on screen long enough to recognise it, in the window where its tune moves (never a stretch of one repeated note), cut at the end of a phrase.
- The buttons of the song being played are on screen whenever a song plays, and no prop ever covers them.
- Melodies and buttons are captured from the running product, never redrawn or synthesised from memory.
- A seasonal or meme sound continues with the part of the song that follows it in the meme (Mariah's "It's time" leads into the up-tempo verse, not the pre-chorus).
- No Megalovania or Sans for now ("esta muy quemado").

## Text on screen

- Song count is never exact: "Play 300+ songs", "Learn over 300 songs".
- The URL is visible the whole film and says it is a website: `ocarinahero.app · free website, no download`; last frame, large: `free in your browser: ocarinahero.app`. Keep it inside the TikTok safe zone (above y = 1520 of 1920; x 150 to 930).
- Packs show a small "1/4" counter in a corner, never a big "Learn 4 songs" header.
- Plain English (Spanish only in the Spanish pack). No em or en dashes.

## Story and staging

- Hook at second 0: a recognisable melody already sounding (memes: an effect with a visible cause, or the quoted sound). No stray effect over an opening melody.
- Where Link appears, Navi flies beside him; a character shown playing has the ocarina at the mouth.
- Props stay coherent from shot to shot (a phone seen from the back in one shot is not seen from the front in the next).
- Props follow the story's logic: a phone nobody is calling does not ring or vibrate; a Nokia shows Snake, not buttons.
- Every universe or beat gets enough time to read; no rushed openings before the hook.
- Ending songs relate to the plot.
- Felt and cloth look of the earlier films; no sewing needle as a motion device.
